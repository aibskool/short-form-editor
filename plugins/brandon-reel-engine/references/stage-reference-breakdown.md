## Design principles that transfer

1. **The frame is a stage and the speaker is its anchor.** A dark canvas carries the idea while the speaker stays on screen in a bottom band; full-frame face returns only at pivots ("It's called…", "So here's what you do…", the CTA).
2. **One argument, one evolving diagram.** Nodes persist and rearrange across sections (triangle → orbit → cards → reframed pair) so the viewer tracks a single mental model instead of reading unrelated cards.
3. **A headline is a chapter marker.** Each stage section opens with a short Title Case takeaway at the top, one phrase contrasted by a bar, italic serif or rule. It is never the spoken caption.
4. **Every element arrives on the word that names it.** Cards, pills, counters and strokes land on the spoken noun; the structure can be shown before it fills.
5. **A small motion vocabulary with fixed meanings.** Pop-with-overshoot = an object arrives; draw-on = a relationship; typing = something being written; count-up = a number; highlighter wipe = read this; dashed orbit/ring = a frame around an idea.
6. **Make the abstract concrete.** An abstract term is paired with a familiar object on screen, and the pairing itself is animated.
7. **Proof lives in UI chrome.** Phones, analytics cards and counters make a claim tangible without a talking point.
8. **One semantic accent.** Near-black, white and grey carry everything; a single accent marks the idea that matters.
9. **Sound mirrors the visual grammar.** Whoosh = cut or slide, pop = arrival, tick = count, typing = writing, low hit = the key concept. Sounds are small and tied to visible events.
10. **Dense, but only a few words at a time.** Big headline, short labels, 1–3 word captions.

## Several ways to apply the style

These are starting grammars, not templates. `motion-system.md` has the components; the build receipt's `motion_report` flags repetition.

| Recipe | Sequence | Signature moves | Use when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Stage Explainer** | Hook headline over the presenter → morph into stage → evolving flow (slots fill on words) → face breather at the pivot → stage proof (window/phone or stat) → face → CTA fan of the real resource pages | Layout morph, flow slots, typed prompt, counter | A mechanism or framework is the point |
| **Presenter-First Pop-ups** (Brandon's default balance) | Presenter throughout; statements, cards and checklists pop over the chest with a local scrim; one stage section for the hardest step; screen proof as a full-frame insert with `expand` or `iris` | Scrim cards, spotlight on a UI row, zoom_blur at the pivot | Personal story or opinion with one mechanism |
| **Equation Hook → Build** | Equation with `?` slots from frame 0 → prompt card types over the chest → stage checklist with seam number badges → stat on the spoken number → CTA `type` | Slots, typing, counters | "Tool + tool = outcome" hooks |
| **Receipts First** | Screen proof opens full frame (highlight + badge) → whip to presenter → compare (old way struck, new way wins) → stage chart → CTA `bubble` | Whip, strike, chart draw | Result-first stories |
| **Myth vs Move** | Statement naming the mistake, then struck → compare → orbit of the tools involved → CTA `stamp` | Strike, orbit, stamp | Contrarian takes |

Rotation rules: change the hook type and the CTA style from the previous reel (record them in `variation.avoid`); vary headline accent styles within a reel (box, serif, underline, color); use at most two stage sections per 30 s unless the mechanism needs more; return to the full-frame face at every real pivot; never stamp the same transition on every cut.
