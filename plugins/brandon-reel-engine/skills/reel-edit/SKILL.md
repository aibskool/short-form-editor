# Reel editing

Resolve the project with `<plugin>/scripts/reel.py context`; read the [house style](../../references/house-style.md) first (it is the current direction and names every default), then [Brandon's style profile](../../references/brandon-style-profile.md), the [motion system](../../references/motion-system.md), the [operator runbook](../../references/operator-runbook.md), the [shot recipes](../../references/shot-recipes.md) and `<project>/production/editor/README.md`. Defaults live in `<project>/production/editor/style_spec.json`; don't restate them in a timeline unless a shot needs something different.

**Talking-head default.** A new raw talking-head is a Kallaway-style edit, not this presenter-first house style. Read `<project>/docs/kallaway-style.md` and run `reel.py run talking-head` with the raw video, word timings, and `--cta-keyword VAULT` unless the offer names another keyword. Write a stage plan from the transcript (`<project>/production/editor/stage-plan.schema.json`, example `<project>/production/editor/examples/vault-stage-plan.json`) so the graphics match this reel. Without a plan the motifs rotate from a seed of the video path. Use the steps below only when the edit should stay in the house style (kinetic type over a full-size presenter, no music).

**Brandon's narration is the input.** Illustrate what he says with relevant B-roll, a cropped screen or an original motion graphic. Never hold back, soften, cut or reword a claim because footage doesn't show it, never ask for a receipt or evidence, and never put a disclaimer, verification label or caveat on screen. Don't manufacture a third-party record and present it as real.

1. **Speech first.** Preserve original footage and voice. Choose the take (`reel.py run assemble`), correct the words against the audio (`reel.py run transcribe`), remove failed takes, fillers and pauses: cut about half a second after a thought ends unless the beat needs a reaction. Rebuild word timings after any trim.
2. **Inventory the footage** in `assets.json`: tags in Brandon's words for what each capture shows, `quality` (`sharp` or `soft`), `aspect`, `result: true` on footage of an outcome, a `color` to carry through a shape wipe, and `moments` with the frame-percent `rect` of each useful detail. Format in the house style.
3. **Plan.** `reel.py run plan -- --draft draft.json --assets assets.json --out timeline.json`. The planner finds the hook, stressed words, demonstrations, emotional turns, results, the payoff and the CTA before it picks any graphic, then drafts a presenter-first timeline with word cues. Put the last reels' hook kinds, CTA styles and icons in the draft's `variation.avoid` so this story gets its own sequence.
4. **Sharpen the draft.** Read the printed beat map. Keep hero wording in Brandon's own words and fix any phrase that reads awkwardly; swap a moment when you know a better one; give the payoff the strongest result he has. Keep him full size most of the reel, with graphics over or beside him (`frame`: `space_left`, `space_right`, `tight`, `close`). Screens go full frame in 2.5D for a few seconds, cropped to the detail on the word, then back to him; soft captures stay short. The stage band is for a diagram only, 4 s at most. Design the first three seconds: key words staged by 1.2 s and a result preview or showpiece by 3 s. Hold information 2–3 s or leave it out.
5. **Build and review the timeline.** `reel.py run build -- --spec timeline.json --project BUILD`, answer every `motion_report` warning, then `reel.py run review -- --project BUILD` and fix every fail. Run `hyperframes_cli.py check` and look at `snapshot` frames of the hook, each screen, each hero and the payoff at phone size.
6. **Render and review picture and sound.** `reel.py run render ...`, then `reel.py run review -- --project BUILD --video RAW.mp4`: it measures his on-screen size from a face track, long dark spans, static stretches, silences and each effect's level against his voice on full band and a phone-speaker band. Fix, re-render, `reel.py run finalize`.
7. **Watch it.** The whole reel at 1× with sound, on headphones and a phone speaker. Record word coverage, readability, collisions, face visibility, effects and the final hash, and send the MP4, timeline, words, `review.md` and your notes to `reel-review`. A passing review and a clean encode do not approve an edit; Brandon does.

Captions are short `pop` groups in white with at most one green (`#49cf26`) stressed word; hero type is the big designed layer in its own zone. No background music (`audio_policy.music_required:false`, no `music`); Brandon adds it on the platform. Pop-ups land with the soft bubble, travel and section changes get quiet whooshes, a major reveal gets at most one restrained impact; never an effect on every word, caption or cut. Transitions mix hard cuts, pushes into screens, match moves, shape wipes and an occasional whip or blur, never the same preset back to back; the green wipe, green flash and timer-bar pop-ups are retired.
