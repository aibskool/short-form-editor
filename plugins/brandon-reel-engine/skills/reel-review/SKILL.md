# Reel quality review

Read [the observed style profile](../../references/brandon-style-profile.md), [quality procedure](../../references/quality-procedure.md) and `<project>/production/editorial-judgment.md`. Review the current hashed MP4 with sound and at phone size, not a planned timeline or a still from another render.

For every spoken claim, name the exact visible proof detail and its limits. A real site capture may prove a demo exists; a deployment progress screen does not prove the final domain was bought. A contextual shot and authored graphic may explain a claim but cannot certify an executed result. Check URLs, owner/source context, crops, labels and qualifications.

Review short spoken captions for complete and accurate word coverage, legible position, and correct emphasis. Review large green/white editorial graphics separately for supported wording, timing, useful hierarchy, animation and collisions with Brandon's face, screen proof, spoken captions and platform controls. Confirm the precise spoken/displayed keyword and actual promised resource. For a ready keyword CTA, Brandon should return face-to-camera.

Play opening and whole edit at 1× with sound. Record useful action and any confusing hold, but do not apply a fixed cut interval, one-second limit or required split. Listen for intact words and the final syllable, audible planned music, effect timing and masking. Inspect the encoded first frame, key proof frames, text entrance/exit frames and the CTA.

For calibration, compare the encoded 20-second Brandon render frame by frame with relevant intervals of [Astra](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdpOfqtgr8a/), [photographer](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdsBCNtB6LW/) and [GovDeals](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdkZiwiuDPv/). Align by visual job, not identical timestamp or pixel difference. Preserve timecoded frames, audio observations, discrepancies, repair and final SHA-256. Mark `pending_review` until Brandon has inspected the final version. A model's description, a passing structural checker, or a successful render cannot claim the style matches.
