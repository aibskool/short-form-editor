# Reel quality review

Read [the observed style profile](../../references/brandon-style-profile.md), [quality procedure](../../references/quality-procedure.md) and `<project>/production/editorial-judgment.md`. Review the current hashed MP4 with sound and at phone size, not a planned timeline or a still from another render.

For every spoken beat, judge whether the visible treatment is understandable and engaging. Take Brandon's statement as given. Do not check the claim or require supporting evidence, and do not downgrade an edit because a supplied capture does not prove a spoken event. Review actual crops, legibility and pacing. No disclaimers or production caveats may appear on screen.

Review short spoken captions for complete and accurate word coverage, legible position and correct emphasis. Review large green/white editorial graphics separately for timing, useful hierarchy, animation and collisions with Brandon's face, screen captures, spoken captions and platform controls. Require animation of every authored on-screen element and purposeful transitions at relevant boundaries even when the assembly does not list them. Confirm the precise spoken/displayed keyword. For a keyword CTA, Brandon should return face-to-camera.

Play opening and whole edit at 1× with sound. Record useful action and any confusing hold, but do not apply a fixed cut interval, one-second limit or required split. Listen for intact words and the final syllable, absence of background music, effect timing and masking. Inspect the encoded first frame, key visual frames, text entrance/exit frames and the CTA.

For calibration, compare the encoded 20-second Brandon render frame by frame with relevant intervals of [Astra](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdpOfqtgr8a/), [photographer](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdsBCNtB6LW/) and [GovDeals](https://www.instagram.com/p/DdkZiwiuDPv/). Align by visual job, not identical timestamp or pixel difference. Preserve timecoded frames, audio observations, discrepancies, repair and final SHA-256. Mark `pending_review` until Brandon has inspected the final version. A model's description, a passing structural checker, or a successful render cannot claim the style matches.
